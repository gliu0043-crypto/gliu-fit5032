GreenMelbourne Connect — Activity information

This file is a sample attachment for the FIT5032 coursework website.
The activities shown on the site are fictional demonstration activities.

Before a community planting session:
- Confirm the activity date and suburb with the organiser.
- Bring a reusable water bottle, a hat and closed shoes.
- Follow the organiser's instructions when using tools.
- Ask the organiser about accessibility or support needs.

Thank you for supporting urban greening and biodiversity.
